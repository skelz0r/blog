title: Privacy
description: What this blog collects about you, which is almost nothing.
---

**Newsletter.** If you subscribe, your email address is stored by
[Buttondown](https://buttondown.com/legal/privacy), which sends the emails.
That is the only personal data collected here. You confirm your
subscription by email, and every email has a one-click unsubscribe link.
Opens and clicks are not tracked. The page you subscribed from is recorded,
to know which articles bring readers.

**Statistics.** Page views are counted with a self-hosted
[Umami](https://umami.is), without cookies and without storing your IP
address. It records the page, the referrer and the `ref` parameter of
links shared on social networks, nothing that identifies you.

**Nothing else.** No ads, no third-party trackers, no fonts or scripts
loaded from other sites.

To be removed from the list, use the unsubscribe link of any email.
